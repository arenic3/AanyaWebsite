Writing

Exhibition Testimony #7: Wake at Fitzrovia Gallery - Asiimov Baker

Aanya Mukhtyar (b.2003) is a British Indian artist based in London. Her paintings illustrate her interest in the relationship between natural light and colour. She uses sunlight as a tool for evoking feelings of transcendence and believes that as a source for all life, sunlight brings viewers instinctively closer to one another and to themselves; a key aim for her during such a divisive period of british contemporary life.

She pulls cross cultural experience to create dream-like blends of both her Indian heritage and British upbringing: a fusion synonymous with herself, and so explores the innumerable facets of diasporic identity today.
